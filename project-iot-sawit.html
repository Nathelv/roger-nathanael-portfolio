IoT Smart Sawit Plantation

An IoT-based smart plantation solution designed to support monitoring and decision-making in palm plantation management.

Project TypeAcademic Group Project
CourseSmart Application
RoleTeam Member
Achievement🏆 1st Place

The Problem

Palm plantation management often relies on manual monitoring, which makes it hard to track conditions consistently and to make timely, data-informed decisions across a large area.

The Solution

We designed an IoT-based system combining sensor-based monitoring with technology-driven analysis to give clearer, real-time insight for plantation management and decision-making.
