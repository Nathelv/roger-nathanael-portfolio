KOMPAS

An academic project management platform designed to help students and lecturers manage academic projects.

Project TypeAcademic Group Project
CourseInformation Systems Project Management (ISPM)
PeriodJanuary – June 2026 · 7 members
RoleBusiness Analyst / Secretary

The Problem

For BINUS students, academic work is spread across individual tasks, group projects, and exam schedules — and it's hard to track in one place. Lecturers also have limited visibility into each student's individual contribution within group work.

The Solution

KOMPAS is a concept for a web-based academic project management platform for BINUS students. It provides a unified dashboard to track Daily Tasks, Group Projects, and Exam Schedules, uses Kanban boards with Agile Scrum, enables real-time collaboration, and improves lecturer visibility into individual contributions.

My Contribution

As Business Analyst / Secretary, I focused on business analysis, project documentation, coordination, requirements and project-planning support, and secretary responsibilities.

Project Management Activities

Project CharterWork Breakdown StructureProject SchedulingGantt ChartCritical PathRisk ManagementProcurement ManagementQuality AssuranceDocumentation

Methodology

Agile Scrum with 2-week sprints. Risk management identified 35 risks, including Scope Creep, Critical Defects at Late Stage, Real-Time Sync Failure, Sprint Schedule Delay, and Academic Data Security Breach. The concept also planned real-time synchronization using WebSocket.

Requirement→ Planning→ Sprint Development→ Review→ Delivery

Business Case

The project's business case projected an initial investment of Rp 15,000,000, an approximate 5-year ROI of 451%, and a payback period of 1.5 years.

These are business-case projections for the project, not realized financial results.

What I Learned

  • How Agile Scrum organizes work into sprints and keeps a team coordinated.
  • Business analysis and requirements/project planning — from Project Charter and WBS to scheduling, risk, and quality management.
  • Documentation and coordination as the backbone that keeps a project team aligned.
